# Solara Bay

[![rank](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/ifulchina-ops/gta6-01/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)
[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/ifulchina-ops/gta6-01/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)

An open-world crime game for the [gta6-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01): Rico rolls into Solara Bay, a sun-soaked city of its own, and runs six jobs to take it. Steal cars and drive, outrun the police under a five-star wanted system, fight the Vipers, and follow the story — all offline, from static files.

Play it: serve `game/` with any static server and open `game/index.html`. WASD to walk and drive, E to steal a car, click to shoot, P to pause. Progress saves in the browser. Six story jobs, five wanted stars, one city to take.
